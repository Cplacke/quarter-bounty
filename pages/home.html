Quarter Bounties

Welcome, Hunter.
The Guild is always searching for elusive quarters still hiding in circulation. Every unmarked coin you recover helps complete the record—and earns you a bounty.
Current Guild Rates:
  • $1.00 Bounty — Quarters in Almost Uncirculated condition.
  • 50¢ Bounty — Quarters in Fair condition.
Inspect every coin that crosses your path. A forgotten quarter in a pocket, a handful of change from the market, or the bottom of an old coin jar could all conceal your next reward.
Take up the hunt, claim your bounty, and may fortune favor your collection.
